## Yihe Yang, Ph.D.

Research Associate, Department of Population and Quantitative Health Sciences, Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine.

Statistician working on **causal inference, statistical genetics, and high-dimensional methods**, with applications to Mendelian randomization, gene prioritization, and personalized treatment policies.
